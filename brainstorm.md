The Niche Cheatsheet: A quick reference guide or interactive FAQ for some hobbies I know well.


## By AI:

---

### 1. Aviation: Flight Deck Checklist & V-Speed Calculator

> **What is it?:** Minimalist cockpit tool.

* **What it does:** A sleek interactive checklist for pre-flight routines (e.g., Cessna 172 startup steps) where clicking items toggles switches visually. You can also add a simple inputs section where users type in aircraft weight to calculate key "V-speeds" (takeoff/landing speeds).

---

### 2. D&D: The "One-Click" NPC / Shop Generator

> **What is it?:** Instant DM aid for quick sessions.

* **What it does:** A single-button generator that outputs a random Tavern, NPC, or Magic Shop item. Clicking "Generate" pulls a random name, personality trait, and secret inventory item from defined arrays and displays them in a stylized card.

---

### 3. Minecraft (Life Series): Session Hearts & Boogeyman Tracker

> **What is it?:** Fan dashboard for Grian's Life Series (3rd Life, Last Life, etc.).

* **What it does:** An interactive scoreboard mockup where you can click players to decrease/increase their life count (turning them from Green $\rightarrow$ Yellow $\rightarrow$ Red) or click a "Roll Boogeyman" button to randomly select a secret player.

---

### 4. Harry Potter: Hogwarts Spell Dueling Simulator

> **What is it?:** Rock Paper Scissors.

* **What it does:** A classic rock-paper-scissors style spell duel (e.g., *Expelliarmus* beats *Stupefy*, *Stupefy* beats *Protego*, *Protego* beats *Expelliarmus*). You select a spell, the AI wizard casts a random spell, and the app tracks your win streak and wizarding rank.
